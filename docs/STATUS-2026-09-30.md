- **Text-completion position cue:** GRE 015, 016 and 017 put the correct word
  first in every blank. `scripts/normalise-option-order.ts` now shuffles
  text-completion blanks; re-version these, run it with `--ids=`, fix their
  accessibility text, and re-solve blind.
- **Numeric order:** six published choice sets are not in ascending order
  because the normaliser used to read `\frac{9}{2}` as 92 (fixed): Bocconi 010,
  SAT 022, ACT 013, TIL-A 507, TOLC-E 709, TOLC-F 702. Cosmetic; the key is
  unaffected.
- **Answer-length cue** (the key is the longest choice far more often than
  chance): fixed and re-reviewed for Bocconi, SAT and TIL-A. The LSAT, ACT,
  GMAT, Bocconi Law and TOLC-E banks are being fixed in a separate session.
  Two new Bocconi critical-thinking items, 434 and 441, are still flagged by
  `scripts/review-tools/cue-scan.mjs` and need their choices rebalanced (then

  about 36% hard and GRE Verbal at about 38% hard, below the 50% target. The
  next wave should be hard and stretch items only.
- **GRE essay:** `scripts/apply-review.ts` has no path for essay items, so an
  Analyze an Issue prompt cannot be published yet; one unlocks the GRE

## How content gets published

1. Author (state `in_review`), following `docs/AUTHORING-BRIEF.md`.
2. Independent critic: solves first, fixes prose, audits difficulty, lists
   quarantines. Always before the blind export.
3. Damage, render and cue checks; `normalise-option-order.ts --exam=…`; then
   bring `accessibilityText` into the new order.
4. `export-review-batch.ts`; two blind solvers per batch, working only from
   the blind file.
5. Publish only when both agree and neither flags ambiguity; confirm no
   solver-visible text changed since the export; `apply-review.ts`;
   `content-gaps.ts`.

The checks and helpers are in `scripts/review-tools/` (see its README). On
Windows, Git Bash collapses backslashes even inside quoted heredocs: write any
content or script containing TeX with an editor, never through the shell.
