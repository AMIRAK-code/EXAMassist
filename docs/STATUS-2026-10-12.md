## How it was reviewed

The usual pipeline (`docs/STATUS-2026-09-30.md`, "How content gets published"): authors wrote to `docs/AUTHORING-BRIEF.md` with `in_review` state; damage, render and cue scans; `normalise-option-order.ts` (wave-8 ids only, leaving the wave-7 held items' recorded order intact); `export-review-batch.ts`; two independent blind solvers per batch working only from the blind file; `build-verdicts.mjs` and `apply-review.ts`, after confirming the solver-visible text had not changed since the export.

- All 238 items: both solves matched the key; none was flagged ambiguous; none quarantined.
- One SAT student-produced-response item was answered "1.2" by one solver and "6/5" by the other; the key accepts both, and the string comparison in `build-verdicts.mjs` reported a split. It was recorded as agreement with both answers kept in the notes.
- Fixed and re-solved before publication: TOLC-F 957 (stem miscounted the patient groups); SAT 533 and 534 (key over 30% longer than every other choice; choices reworded to similar lengths, items reverted to v2 and solved again).
- Six items were relabelled hard → medium where solvers judged them routine.
- `accessibilityText` choice lists were brought into the post-normalisation option order (205 items; a pure reordering of that one field).

## Known gaps

- The Bocconi config's penalty is −0.2 for four-option items; the authoring prompts for this wave said −0.33. It changes nothing in the items (all four options), only the rationale the authors had in mind for distractors.
- `npm run e2e` in this container needs Playwright pointed at `/opt/pw-browsers/chromium` (the pinned Playwright expects a newer headless shell). With that, `tests/e2e/new-exams*.spec.ts` pass, including `/practice/tolc-i`.
- New tool: `scripts/review-tools/skill-coverage.ts [exam...]` lists items per declared skill, including skills with none.
