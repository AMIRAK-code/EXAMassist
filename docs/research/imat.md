**Version covered:** the IMAT for admission to the English-taught degrees in Medicine and Surgery (LM-41), Dentistry and Dental Prosthodontics (LM-46) and Veterinary Medicine (LM-42), academic year 2026/27  
**Verified on:** 2026-11-03, first-hand: the decree, its annexes and tables were downloaded from mur.gov.it and their text extracted; the key clauses (format, time, scoring, threshold, date, rankings) were re-read in the extracted text in a separate pass. No third-party summary was relied on.  
**Delivery:** in person, on paper, on one day, at Italian universities and at venues abroad [1, 2].  
**Status:** the 2026/27 test was held on **29 September 2026**. Nothing has been published for 2027/28 yet (checked 3 November 2026).

## The 2025 reform and the English-taught degrees — [9, 10, 1]

- The reform that replaced the entrance test with a *semestre filtro* does not cover these degrees. D.Lgs. 71/2025, art. 8 c. 3: "Le disposizioni del presente decreto non si applicano ai corsi di studio erogati in lingua inglese presso le università statali e non statali legalmente riconosciute". D.M. 941/2026, art. 1 c. 3 repeats it for 2026/27.
- D.M. 1005/2026, art. 1 c. 1: for 2026/27 admission to the English-taught degrees "avviene a seguito di superamento di un'unica e apposita prova d'esame disciplinata dal presente decreto". MUR calls it "IMAT" in its notices [6].

## Format — [1, 3]

Art. 2 c. 2: "La prova di ammissione consiste nella soluzione di sessanta quesiti che presentano cinque opzioni di risposta, tra cui il candidato deve individuarne una soltanto". Art. 2 c. 4: "Per lo svolgimento della prova è assegnato un tempo di 100 minuti."

| Area | Questions |
| --- | --- |
| Reading skills and knowledge acquired in studies | 4 |
| Logical reasoning and problems | 5 |
| Biology | 23 |
| Chemistry | 15 |
| Physics and mathematics | 13 |
| **Total** | **60 in 100 minutes** |

- Same wording and numbers as 2025/26 [11].
- One test for all three degrees, identical at every venue (art. 2 c. 1).
- **New in 2026/27:** at registration a candidate chooses Medicine and Surgery / Dentistry **or** Veterinary Medicine; the choice becomes irrevocable when registration closes (art. 1 c. 2). In 2025 a candidate could list both.
- **In English only.** Allegato A: "tutti i tipi di domande saranno formulati in lingua inglese, anche ricorrendo al linguaggio simbolico."
- **On paper** (Allegato 1, points 8–9): a question booklet and an answer sheet marked with an X in black pen supplied at the venue; one correction per question; a deliberate blank is marked as such. Allegato 1 bans phones, tablets, smartwatches "o altra strumentazione similare", manuals, notes, and any pen or pencil of one's own. It does not name calculators; nothing allows one, and candidates write only with what the venue hands out.
- Nothing divides the 100 minutes or fixes an order, so candidates move freely through the booklet. That follows from the paper format; no clause states it.

## Scoring and ranking — [1, 2]

- Art. 3 c. 4: at most "novanta (90) punti": "1,5 punti per ogni risposta esatta", "meno 0,4 (- 0,4) punti per ogni risposta errata", "0 punti per ogni risposta omessa".
- **Threshold** (art. 3 c. 2): EU candidates and non-EU candidates resident in Italy are eligible only with "un punteggio minimo pari a venti (20) punti". One clause covers all three degrees, Veterinary Medicine included.
- Non-EU candidates resident abroad (art. 3 c. 3) need only "un punteggio superiore a zero (0)", and compete for their own places in the ranking of the university they pre-enrolled at.
- **Two national rankings** (art. 3 c. 5): "due distinte graduatorie nazionali", one for Medicine and Surgery with Dentistry, one for Veterinary Medicine.
- **Ties** (art. 3 c. 6), in order: the higher score in biology, then chemistry, physics and mathematics, logical reasoning, reading and knowledge; then a recognised English certificate (Allegato 3, B2–C2, or a year of schooling in English); then a disability of at least 66%; then the younger candidate.
- Expected value of a blind guess with five options: 1.5 × 0.2 − 0.4 × 0.8 = −0.02 points (our arithmetic, not the decree's).

## Who runs it — [1, 2]

- MUR sets the rules by decree. **CINECA** writes the questions, prepares the test packs, runs the test in Italy and abroad and computes the scores (decree recitals; Allegato 1, points 1–2). Universities run the rooms and publish their own calls at least 30 days before the test (art. 8).
- Venues 2026 [4, 6]: 18 Italian universities (Bari, Milano Statale, Milano-Bicocca, Roma La Sapienza, Roma Tor Vergata, Napoli Vanvitelli, Napoli Federico II, Bologna, Torino, Pavia, Parma, Siena, Messina, Padova, Politecnica delle Marche, Cagliari, Catania, and Firenze from 26 August) and venues in 25 countries. A candidate in Italy sits the test at their first-choice university.

## Syllabus — [3]

Allegato A of 2026 is word for word the 2025 one. Headings: reading skills and general knowledge (comprehension of English texts; history, institutions, citizenship); logical reasoning and problems; biology (chemistry of living things, the cell, cell cycle, reproduction and heredity, genetics, mutations and evolution, biotechnology, anatomy and physiology, bioenergetics); chemistry (matter, gases, atomic structure and periodic table, bonding, inorganic nomenclature, stoichiometry, solutions and equilibria, kinetics, redox, acids and bases, organic chemistry); mathematics (algebra, functions, geometry and trigonometry, probability and statistics); physics (units and vectors, kinematics, dynamics, fluids, thermodynamics, electricity and magnetism).

## What the build does with this

- `src/lib/exams/configs/imat.ts` (version `2026.11`): 60 questions in one 100-minute block in the published mix, five options, +1.5 / 0 / −0.4, out of 90. The ranking threshold of 20 is stated, not projected: a raw score does not say whether a place is won, which depends on the ranking against each year's places.
- The full simulation is offered, labelled a close approximation: it is on screen, not on paper, and the questions are ours. It is always in English. Practice and the diagnostic may be taken in Italian, as a study aid.
- The hub `/exams/imat` and its Italian version state what is confirmed for 2026/27 and that 2027/28 is not published.
- Re-verify when the 2027/28 decree appears (usually early August): format, scoring, threshold, date, places and syllabus.
