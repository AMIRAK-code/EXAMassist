**Version covered:** a.y. 2026/27 admissions  
**Verified on:** 2026-09-29, first-hand, by reading the official documents in full (the PDFs were downloaded and their text extracted; no summary was relied on)  
**Delivery:** in person, on Politecnico di Torino computers ("All students will take the admission test (TIL test) for academic year 2026/27 in person").

> Every factual row carries a numbered source. Where PoliTo does not publish something, this record says so rather than estimating it.

## A note on naming

There is **no "TIL-E"**. PoliTo's bachelor admission tests are TIL-I (Ingegneria / Engineering), TIL-A (Architettura / Architecture), TIL-D (Design) and TIL-P (Pianificazione / Planning) [2]. A request for "TIL-E" is read as the engineering test, TIL-I, and the site uses PoliTo's own name so learners can find it.

## TIL-I (Engineering)

### Structure — art. 7 of [1], confirmed on [2]

| # | Section | Questions | Minutes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Matematica / Mathematics | 16 | 36 |
| 2 | Comprensione del testo e logica / Reading comprehension and logical reasoning | 10 | 20 |
| 3 | Fisica / Physics | 10 | 22 |
| 4 | Conoscenze tecniche di base / Basic technical knowledge | 6 | 12 |
| | **Total** | **42** | **90** |

- "Per ciascun quesito sono proposte 5 risposte contraddistinte con le lettere A, B, C, D, E, una sola delle quali è esatta." [1, art. 7.2]
- Individual test, different for each candidate, drawn automatically and at random; "Tutti i test generati hanno difficoltà equivalente." Available in Italian and English. [1, art. 7.1]
- Basic technical knowledge: "3 questions on Representation and 3 questions on Computer Science". [2]

### Scoring — art. 8 of [1]

- +1 correct, 0 unanswered, −0.25 wrong. "Il punteggio è convertito in centesimi e calcolato fino alla seconda cifra decimale."
- A Mathematics score below 30% of the section maximum (below 4.8/16) assigns remedial obligations (OFA) to an admitted candidate. [1, art. 8.3]

### Thresholds — art. 3 of [1]

- Ranking threshold: **30/100** for the Engineering-area programmes; any score above 0 for Tecnologie per l'industria manifatturiera.
- Guarantee threshold: **60/100**, "tale punteggio consente di immatricolarsi al corso di laurea di prima preferenza".
- SAT alternative: at least 650 in each of Evidence-Based Reading and Writing and Math; ranking only, no guarantee threshold. [1, arts. 3.2, 12]

### Attempts, validity, conduct

- "È possibile sostenere il TIL-I per un massimo di 3 volte e solo una volta per sessione. Nel caso di ripetizione del test, sarà considerato valido il risultato migliore ottenuto." [1, art. 5.4]
- The score is valid only for enrolment in a.y. 2026/27. [1, Premessa, point 5]
- No calculators, notes or personal sheets; only the blank sheets and pen or pencil provided. A non-programmable calculator and extra time (up to 30% for SpLD, 50% for disability) for certified candidates only. [1, arts. 6, 9]

### Macro-topics — [2]

- **Mathematics:** natural, relative and rational numbers; real numbers, radicals and exponents; statistics and probability; plane and basic spatial geometry, areas and volumes; monomial and polynomial algebra; analytic geometry, exponentials and logarithms; goniometry and trigonometry; elementary graphs and plane regions; algebraic, irrational and absolute-value equations and inequalities; exponential, logarithmic and trigonometric equations and inequalities.
- **Reading comprehension and logic:** excerpts (scientific, popular, historical, sociological) whose answers "must be deduced exclusively from the content of the excerpt"; logic questions on orders, quantifiers, logical deductions, elementary logic problems, negation of statements.
- **Physics:** vectors; units of measurement; kinematics in one and two dimensions; forces and point dynamics; work-energy; calorimetry; thermodynamics; electrostatics; electrical circuits.
- **Basic technical knowledge:** representation (relating objects to their 2D and 3D representation, graphic-technical coding); computer science (programs, variables, assignment, testing vs assignment, operators, logical operators, if-then-else, for/while/repeat-until, cycles, bit/byte/transistor, exact arithmetic, an example language such as C or Python).

### Not published (unverified)

- Movement between questions, or changing answers, **within** an open section.
- Whether sections must be taken in the listed order, or can be closed early.
- How the 10 reading-and-logic questions divide: [2] describes two excerpts of three questions plus five logic questions, which is **11**, not the 10 that [1] states. The call is the legal document; the split is left open.
- Whether the reported score can be negative.

## TIL-A (Architecture)

### Structure — art. 7 of [3]

| # | Section | Questions | Minutes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Reading comprehension | 10 | 20 |
| 2 | Prior school knowledge: History (including History of Art) and General Knowledge | 10 | 20 |
| 3 | Logic reasoning | 10 | 20 |
| 4 | Drawing and Representation | 10 | 20 |
| 5 | Physics and Mathematics | 10 | 20 |
| | **Total** | **50** | **100** |

- "Applicants must answer the questions of each section in 20 minutes. The total time for the TIL-A test is set at 100 minutes. Each question has 5 possible answers, only one of which is correct." [3, art. 7.3]
- Taken in English or Italian, chosen at registration. [3, art. 7.2]
- Content set nationally by Annex A of Ministerial Decree 706/2026 [4]; PoliTo's English rendering of it "has no legal effects" [2].
- Sessions: Tuesday 21 and Wednesday 22 July 2026. [3]

### Scoring and ranking — arts. 8 and 11 of [3]

- +1 correct, 0 unanswered, −0.25 wrong; "The maximum test score is fifty (50) points"; "The score is converted to hundredths" (no formula given).
- Below 3/10 in Physics and Mathematics: remedial obligations (OFA) for an admitted student.
- **No pass mark.** Applicants are ranked in descending order of score; ties go to section scores in the order Logic reasoning, Reading comprehension, Drawing and Representation, Physics and Mathematics, Prior school knowledge; then to the younger applicant. Places are offered down the ranking. Applicants who answer no question are not ranked. Separate lists for EU and equivalent applicants and for non-EU applicants residing abroad. [3, arts. 10–11]
- No calculators or personal materials; extra time and a non-programmable calculator for certified candidates only. [3, arts. 5, 9]

### Macro-topics — [2], from Annex A of [4]

- **Reading comprehension:** texts of various kinds and purposes — scientific or narrative, classical or contemporary, current news, journal articles.
- **Logical reasoning:** reasoning consistent with premises in symbolic or verbal form, including abstract problems.
- **Prior school knowledge:** placing historical-cultural phenomena, including artistic and architectural events, works and movements, in space and time; national and international institutions; legal and economic issues and citizenship.
- **Drawing and representation:** analysing graphs, drawings and iconic representations and their correspondence with objects; plans, elevations, axonometric projections.
- **Mathematics and physics:** number sets and arithmetic, algebra, Euclidean geometry, fundamentals of analytic geometry, probability, statistics and trigonometry; elementary mechanics and statics; thermodynamics concepts; electrostatics and direct-current circuits.

### Not published (unverified)

- Movement within an open section; the delivery order of the five areas.
- The formula behind "converted to hundredths"; whether a negative score is reported.

## What the build does with this

- Both tests are modelled as consecutive sections with their own clocks and **no return to a closed section**; movement within a section is allowed and disclosed as unverified. Full simulations are labelled **approximation**.
- TIL-I readiness converts a raw /42 to the reported /100 and compares it with the **published** 30 and 60 thresholds.
- TIL-A readiness reports the 50-point score and states that there is no pass mark, only a ranking.
- The site cannot yet render figures, so Representation and Drawing items pose the same spatial problems through tables and text, and the fidelity notes say so.
