# Physics II for Computer Engineering

Checked 23 November 2026 for the English-language 2026/27 offering.

The year-two Computer Engineering curriculum lists *Electromagnetism and Circuit theory*. The study plan assigns 5 CFU to Physics II and 7 to Circuit Theory. Course codes can vary by degree listing. The course data cites the 01URLYF syllabus. [Study plan](https://didattica.polito.it/pls/portal30/sviluppo.offerta_formativa_2019.vis?p_cds=561&p_coorte=2026&p_sdu=37)

The Physics II module reviews electrostatics and conduction, proceeds to steady magnetic fields, induction and Maxwell’s equations, then studies electromagnetic waves, energy, momentum and polarisation. Its prerequisites include Physics I and calculus. The practice problems and polarisation experiment are UniExamer teaching material.
