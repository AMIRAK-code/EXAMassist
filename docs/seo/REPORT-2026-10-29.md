## 1. Most important findings (production, 29 October 2026)

Evidence: a crawl of the live sitemap and every internal link it led to (`node scripts/seo-crawl.mjs https://uniexamer.com`: 155 sitemap URLs, about 250 URLs crawled), the live HTML, Bing results, and the code. Search Console and Bing performance data were **not** read: the built-in browser is not signed in to Google, and on 27 October both consoles were still processing (see the 27 October audit).

Confirmed problems:

1. **The rewritten Bocconi sample-questions article was not live.** `/guides/bocconi-admission-test-sample-questions` returned 404; the draft existed only as uncommitted work.
2. **The home page's Organization and WebSite markup had no `alternateName`**, although the code comment said it did: the call site never passed the names. No `sameAs` either (PR #3, which adds the profiles, was not deployed).
3. **Translated frames around English content were advertised as translations.** Every exam hub except Bocconi, every format guide and every guide article show English content under a translated interface, yet each language version was its own canonical, carried five-language hreflang and was listed in the sitemap: 5 duplicate titles and descriptions per page.
4. **Snippets too long**: home descriptions 292–340 characters, exam directory 199–246, about pages 226–300, hub descriptions 200+ (the full exam tagline); 51 titles over 65 characters, and the home title 73–85 depending on language. The exam directory's description omitted TOLC and TIL.
5. **The header overflowed on phones**: 31 px at 391 px wide and 63 px at 360 px, so every page scrolled sideways on small phones.
6. **Brand recognition is early.** Bing shows uniexamer.com first for "uniexamer", but with the old "Admission test preparation, verified against official sources" title and the long description. A general web search engine returned nothing for "UniExamer". Unrelated look-alikes appear for the brand query (uniexames.com.br, uniexam.net).

Checked and sound: robots.txt (public pages open, private areas excluded, sitemap advertised), HTTPS, `/premium` → `/pricing` (308), www and exam.assist365.app → apex (301, via `next.config.ts`), no localhost or preview hosts in production HTML, every sitemap URL answering 200, private routes `noindex`. `/account` returned 500 once under load (Workers Free CPU limit) and 307 to sign-in on recheck; it is disallowed in robots.txt and not in the sitemap.

Hypotheses (not proven): Google's slow pickup of the hubs is discovery lag after the 27 October domain move rather than a block (the live URL test passed on 27 October). Bing's old snippet is a recrawl lag.

Needs more data: queries, CTR and positions (empty consoles); Core Web Vitals (no field data, per the 27 October audit).

## 4. The Bocconi article

`/guides/bocconi-admission-test-sample-questions`, same URL as the draft.

- **Scope and facts.** States that it is for the undergraduate Online Bocconi Test, with one item for the Law test, and sends master's applicants to the right hub. The structure (Maths 24, Reading 11, Numerical reasoning 6, Critical thinking 9; Law 5/11/6/18/10), timing, scoring (+1, 0, −0.2, −0.33 on three-option critical thinking), the reading-passage themes and the two critical-thinking question types were re-read on Bocconi's official test page on 29 October 2026 and are cited.
- **Questions.** Eleven instead of four, chosen to cover the question types Bocconi describes, not to reach a length: percentages, an absolute-value inequality, conditional probability and a logarithm equation (domain check); two questions on one economics passage (explicit and implicit meaning); two tables with information to ignore (rates vs totals, percentage points vs relative change; shares of different totals); a three-option true / false / cannot-be-deduced item and a data-and-statements item; an assumption item for the Law test's verbal reasoning. Four options where Bocconi publishes no count, three where it describes three outcomes.
- **Checking.** Every question was solved independently: arithmetic, units and percentages, the passage's support for the key, uniqueness of the key, and each distractor. One draft item repeated the set-up of a TOLC-E bank question (30 students, 18 French, 5 neither) and was replaced, so publishing it spoils no scored session; a test now guards this.
- **Answers.** Native `<details>`: closed until opened, keyboard and screen-reader operable without script, present in the HTML. Each gives the answer, the working, why every wrong option tempts, and what carries over. A table of contents links every question.
- **Honesty.** Original, AI-assisted examples; not past questions, not calibrated, not a score predictor; not in the question bank. "About this page" states the authorship, the date, that every answer was re-derived and that **no human subject expert has reviewed them**, and how to report a mistake.
- **Links.** Bocconi hub, format guide, negative-marking guide, preparation plan, undergraduate-vs-Law guide, Bocconi's own simulation and topic list.
- **Mobile.** Checked at 360 px: no sideways scrolling, fractions and tables readable.

## 6. Deployment and discovery

**Deployed** on 29 October 2026 with `npm run cf:deploy` from the clean checkout `../examer-cf-deploy`: Worker version `99b66b54` (commit `942bba3`), then `841df53b` (`0af4cea`, shorter snippets found by the post-release crawl). Both saved all 166 public pages as static files. **Production ran `841df53b`** (this branch at `0af4cea`) until the final release described below, **not main**: main's human-approval and pricing changes are not live, and this branch must be merged into main before main is deployed.

**Incident, 15:35–16:20 UTC.** A third pass (`9e17c37`, seven shorter descriptions) first failed with a Cloudflare API 504, then on retry went live as `69d100b4` while its static-page snapshot stalled on Free-plan throttling (error 1102, seen as 503). For about 45 minutes most public pages answered 503 intermittently, the home page and sitemap included. The deploy was stopped and production rolled back to `841df53b` with `wrangler rollback` (run with `CI=true`, since Wrangler otherwise waits at an interactive prompt despite `-y`). All key pages answered 200 afterwards. A second attempt at 18:07 UTC stalled the same way and was rolled back at 18:23 (users saw error 1102 for about 16 minutes).

**Fix, and final release.** `cf:deploy` now uploads the build as a version with no traffic, saves the public pages from that version's preview URL, and only then deploys (`3c6a728`, also on main as `95c452f`; README, "Cloudflare Workers"). Preview URLs had to be switched on once for the Worker (`previews_enabled`, with workers.dev itself still off). At 19:28–19:52 UTC the branch at `3c6a728` (code identical to `9e17c37`) was released this way as Worker version **`d511b971`**: the snapshot again waited out throttling for several minutes, but the live site answered 200 on every check throughout, because visitors stayed on `841df53b` until the version with all 166 pages was deployed. Production now runs `d511b971`.

Verified live on the first version, and again on `841df53b` after the rollback: the home page title, `alternateName` and four `sameAs` profiles; the sample-questions guide (200, 11 answer blocks, self-canonical); localized copies of English-only pages canonical to English with only en/x-default; the Italian Bocconi hub self-canonical with five-language hreflang; sitemap 73 URLs, all 200; http, www and exam.assist365.app 301 to the apex; `/premium` 308 to `/pricing`; an unknown page 404 with `noindex`; no localhost or preview hosts; Bocconi hub and practice entry links working. The post-release crawl found no duplicate titles or descriptions among canonical pages, no missing H1s and no orphan pages. Remaining long snippets: four guide descriptions and the Turkish, Spanish and Polish Bocconi descriptions (fixed in the undeployed `9e17c37`), and the pricing and terms pages, which the pricing branch rewrites. Dynamic, robots-disallowed pages (practice, checkout, account) still time out or return 500 at times under crawl load: the Workers Free plan's CPU limit, a hosting decision rather than an SEO defect.

**Submitted for discovery:** IndexNow accepted the 73 sitemap URLs (HTTP 200, which confirms receipt only).

**Not done:** Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools. The sitemap refresh and indexing requests (home page, Bocconi hub, sample-questions guide) need a signed-in Google session. **Confirmed indexed:** nothing new; check URL inspection in a few days.

## 7. Blockers and open items

- Search Console and Bing reports need the owner's sign-in in the browser used for this work.
- Translating the TOLC and TIL hubs needs a fluent reviewer (EXAM-INTENT-MAP.md §3).
- An "expert reviewed" statement needs a real, named, qualified reviewer (CONTENT-MAINTENANCE.md, authority plan).

- The language redirect on static pages (a remembered or browser language moves a visitor off an English URL) is kept: crawlers are not affected, but Google advises against automatic redirects. Replacing it with a suggestion banner is a product decision.
- This branch must be merged into main before main's next release; expect conflicts in `messages/*/about.json` (editorial standards text) and in the pricing work's Bocconi landing and page dates.
