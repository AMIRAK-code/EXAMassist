## 1. Findings

Evidence: a crawl of production on 12 November (`node scripts/seo-crawl.mjs https://uniexamer.com`, stopped at its 900-page cap; the live sitemap listed 341 URLs), the live HTML and response headers, and the code. Search Console was not read (no access from this session); the latest figures are the owner's baseline in MEASUREMENT-PLAN.md, with data ending 31 October: 10 clicks, 312 impressions, average position 33, and 0 clicks from 143 non-branded impressions.

**Confirmed problems**

1. **44 lesson pages were copies of other lesson pages.** TOLC-E and TOLC-F reuse TOLC-I's shared lessons unchanged; only the practice link and the review record differ (`content/tolc-e/adapt.ts`, `content/tolc-f/shared.ts`). Each copy named itself as canonical and was in the sitemap, so 27 lessons were published at two or three addresses. Measured on `quadratic-equations-and-inequalities`, the three pages share 97–98% of their 5-word sequences; the rest is breadcrumbs. Those 44 copies were 13% of the sitemap's 341 URLs.
2. **Most titles were longer than Google shows.** 276 of the 341 indexable pages crawled had titles over 60 characters, and 258 were over 65. Lessons were the cause: "<lesson> | <course title> | UniExamer", 90 characters at the median. Examples: "How the GRE works: sections, timing, adaptivity and scoring | GRE General Test course | UniExamer" (97), and a Bocconi lesson at 124. Four of the seven course pages were also over 60, even before the site name.
3. **The content for the Italian exams is almost all in English.** 295 of the 341 sitemap URLs are English. The Italian ones are the translated frame pages plus three exam hubs (Bocconi, CISIA TOLC, IMAT). Every course is English only, including about 95 TOLC lesson pages, although the English TOLCs became the CEnT-S in November 2025 (REPORT-2026-11-02.md), so TOLC candidates sit an Italian-language test. **Not changed here:** this needs translation and a fluent reviewer (§5).
4. **`llms.txt` described the courses as "for the GRE, the GMAT and the Bocconi bachelor's admission test"**, three of the seven.

**Checked and sound**

- Every sitemap URL the crawl reached (340 of 341, before its cap) answers 200. The public pages checked are served as static files (`Cache-Control: public, max-age=0, must-revalidate`), answer in about 0.1 s and load about 200 KB of scripts in 10 files.
- `robots.txt` allows the public pages and disallows the private ones in every language. `www`, `http` and `exam.assist365.app` 301 to `https://uniexamer.com`, keeping the path. No `X-Robots-Tag` is sent.
- Every canonical page has exactly one H1 and a description, and no page references a retired host.
- `noindex` is only on sign-in, sign-up and report-question.
- The crawl's 74 redirects (307) are `/practice/*` → `/start/*`, and its 6 failures are localised `/practice` and `/checkout` pages. All of these are disallowed and not in the sitemap.
- English-only pages under `/it`, `/es`, `/tr` and `/pl` name the English page as canonical and list only `en` and `x-default` as alternates. Their `<html lang>` is the interface language, while the article itself carries `lang="en"`. That is correct markup and needs no change.

**Hypothesis, not checked:** the 251 lessons published on 9–10 November, on a domain that moved on 3 November, are probably still largely "Discovered – currently not indexed". Search Console's page indexing report is the place to check this before adding more pages.

## 2. Changes (implemented, not committed)

1. **One canonical address per lesson text.** `canonicalLessonPaths` in `src/lib/courses/index.ts` compares lessons that share a slug, using every field except `practice` and `review`. A lesson identical to one in an earlier course (in course-list order) names that copy as its canonical, which today is TOLC-I for all 27.
   - Only published lessons count. If the first course's copy were still a draft, the next published copy would stand in.
   - Lessons that only share a slug keep their own canonical, because their text differs: GRE and GMAT `probability`, and Bocconi versus TOLC `exponential-and-logarithmic-equations`.
   - A copy rewritten for its own course becomes its own canonical without any further change.
   - The comparison stops early on shared objects. Building the whole map takes 0.7 ms, and a full `sitemap()` call takes about 1.2 ms after that, well inside the Worker's CPU limit.
   - Uses of the map:
     - The lesson page passes the canonical path to `pageAlternates`, so canonical, hreflang and `og:url` all name the original.
     - `sitemap.ts` lists only canonical lessons, taking the sitemap from 341 to 297 URLs.
     - `llms.txt` links each lesson at its canonical address.
   - The copies stay in their courses: same URL, breadcrumbs, previous and next links, and practice link.
2. **Titles within about 60 characters.** `pageTitle(name, ...context)` in `src/lib/seo/title.ts` always keeps the page's own name whole. It then adds each context part and the site name only while the title fits (`TITLE_BUDGET` = 60). When the site name would not fit, it returns `{ absolute }`, so the layout template (`%s | UniExamer`) leaves the site name out. Google shows the site's name above each result anyway.
   - **Pages that use it:** lessons, course pages, the exams, guides and courses indexes, exam hubs (including the Bocconi hub's own metadata), format guides and guides. On hubs, format guides, guides and indexes the wording is unchanged; only the site name is dropped where it does not fit, because some of these titles already have impressions.
   - **Course short names:** a new required `Course.shortTitle` (at most 24 characters, e.g. "TOLC-I course") follows the lesson title when it fits. The full course title used to follow it.
   - **Lesson search titles:** a new optional `Lesson.metaTitle` (at most 60) is the search title for the 18 lessons whose own title is longer than 60. The page's heading keeps `title`.
   - **Lesson titles after the change:** median 54 characters instead of 90, and none over 60 (246 were over 60 before).
   - **Validator rules (`content:validate`):**
     - A course `metaTitle` over 60 is rejected.
     - A `shortTitle` over 24 is rejected.
     - A lesson title over 60 without a `metaTitle` is rejected.
     - A `metaTitle` over 60 is rejected.
   - **Reworded course titles (each course's units unchanged):**

## 3. Validation

- `npm run verify`: typecheck and content validation clean; 64 test files, 1,014 tests passing.
- **New and changed tests:**
  - `tests/unit/page-title.test.ts` covers the title rules and the pages that use `pageTitle`.
  - `tests/unit/courses.test.ts` checks that:
    - the TOLC-E and TOLC-F copies point to TOLC-I;
    - lessons that only share a slug stay their own canonical;
    - every canonical target is an identical, published lesson in an earlier course and is canonical itself;
    - the draft and rewritten-copy cases work;
    - the sitemap and `llms.txt` use canonical addresses;
    - every lesson's search title is 60 characters or less, with the course name wherever it fits;
    - every course page title fits;
    - the `metaTitle` validation rule works.
  - `tests/unit/seo.test.ts` now expects the sitemap without the copies.
- **The tests catch the problem:** with duplicate detection disabled, the canonical tests fail.
- **On `next dev`:**
  - The TOLC-E and TOLC-F copies of `real-numbers-powers-and-radicals`, including `/it/courses/tolc-f/...`, name the TOLC-I page in canonical, hreflang and `og:url`. So does `tolc-f/sine-and-cosine-rules`, a lesson shared with TOLC-I only.
  - The TOLC-I original, `tolc-e/arguments-assumptions-and-evidence`, `gre/probability` and `gmat/probability` keep their own canonical.
  - Rendered titles match section 2 on lessons, course pages, the exams, guides and courses indexes (plus the courses index in Spanish and Turkish), hubs (EN, PL, IT), a format guide and a guide. Lesson headings are unchanged.
- **With production settings (`NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL=https://uniexamer.com`, indexing on):** `sitemap()` lists 297 URLs, 207 of them lessons, and `llms.txt` has no copy addresses.
- **Not run:** `npm run build`, the Playwright suites, the Rich Results Test, and a Cloudflare preview deploy.

## 4. Deployment notes

- **Deploy machine:** follow the release checklist in REPORT-2026-11-02-SEVEN-PRIORITIES.md, building on WSL or Linux and never on Windows.
- **Snapshot:** the copies are still prerendered and saved as static files, because `generateStaticParams` lists every lesson and `savePrerenderedPages` reads the prerender manifest. The snapshot's sitemap-derived list shrinks by 220 paths (44 lessons in five languages); those pages are still saved from the prerender output.
- **After the deploy:**
  1. `curl` a copy, e.g. `/courses/tolc-e/real-numbers-powers-and-radicals`, and check that its canonical is the TOLC-I page.
  2. Check that `sitemap.xml` lists 297 URLs and that a lesson's `<title>` is short.
  3. Resubmit the sitemap in Search Console and run `npm run seo:indexnow`.
  4. A week later, URL-inspect one copy, where Google should report the TOLC-I page as canonical.
- **Unrelated lockfile change:** `package-lock.json` in this working tree also moves `@swc/core` from 1.16.20 to 1.16.22, within next-intl's `~1.16.0`. That is a local fix: 1.16.20's native loader refuses to run on a Windows machine whose drive root grants Authenticated Users Modify. It is unrelated to search, and can go in the same release or its own.

## 5. Decisions for the owners

- **Course titles and lesson search titles:** the wording of the four course `metaTitle`s and the 18 lesson search titles in section 2. Edit any of them freely; the validator only enforces the lengths.
- **Titles left slightly long:** the Polish Bocconi hub (61 characters) and the Italian TOLC hub (62) are over by one or two characters of their own text, and were left as they are.
- **Italian content (finding 3):** which courses to translate first. The suggestion is TOLC-I, then TOLC-E and TOLC-F, because those candidates search in Italian. `contentLocales` in `src/i18n/alternates.ts` would need courses added once a lesson's whole body is translated and reviewed.
