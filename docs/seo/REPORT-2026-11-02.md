## 1. Most important findings

Evidence: the owner's Search Console figures (26–31 October; summarised in MEASUREMENT-PLAN.md), a crawl of the live sitemap (`node scripts/seo-crawl.mjs`: 73 sitemap URLs, 265 crawled), the live HTML, PoliTo's and CISIA's official pages fetched on 2 November, Google Search Central's current documentation, and the code.

**Confirmed problems**

1. **The best-placed page described a test that is being retired.** `/exams/politecnico-di-torino-til` (48 impressions, position 6.5) presented the TIL-I and TIL-A as PoliTo's admission tests. PoliTo's admission test page now says: "Starting from the 2027/2028 academic year, the Politecnico will join the CISIA consortium"; only in-person TOLC@UNI and CEnT@UNI will be accepted; its 23 July 2026 news item names "TOLC per i candidati nazionali e CeNT-S per quelli internazionali"; the 2027/28 calls are "being finalized". Anyone searching now is a 2027/28 applicant. The page gave them no sign of this.
2. **The TOLC page left out two facts a searcher needs.** The English TOLC-E, TOLC-I and TOLC-F were replaced by the CEnT-S from November 2025 (CISIA's CEnT page), so the TOLC is an Italian-language test; and no 2027 calendar or regulation is published (`Regolamento-TOLC-2027.pdf` is 404; CISIA announces structural changes by 31 December, Regolamento 2026 art. 1.2).
3. **Every page shipped every interface text.** The locale layout's `NextIntlClientProvider` had no `messages`, so all twelve catalogues (208 KB of English JSON, account, billing and player texts included) were inlined into each page: live HTML 271–472 KB, 56–67 KB compressed, on pages whose client components need about 3 KB of it.
4. **robots.txt only covered English paths.** `Disallow: /practice/` does not match `/it/practice/`, so localized practice, start, free-test, checkout, sign-in and account URLs were crawlable. The crawl reached them through links on public pages; they timed out (76 requests, consistent with the Workers Free CPU limit) or answered 500 (`/checkout` in four languages; not reproduced locally, where it redirects to sign-up). Crawling them spends crawl capacity on pages that are `noindex` anyway and shows search engines server errors. (The crawl itself loaded the Worker: dynamic routes answered 503 for several minutes afterwards; static public pages were not affected.)
5. **Hub dates ignored hub edits.** Sitemap `lastmod` and Article `dateModified` for exam hubs used only the configuration's `verifiedOn` (TIL: 29 September), so a substantive change to a hub could never be signalled honestly.
6. **Organic visitors reaching practice could not be counted from exam pages.** Views had a coarse source; clicks into practice were counted only from guides.
7. **Sample questions converge with the bank.** The first draft of five TOLC samples contained three items whose set-up and numbers already exist in the bank (a TIL-I equation word for word, a TOLC-F mean problem with the same numbers, a TOLC-E negation); the wording check caught only one. Both are AI-assisted and drift to textbook set-ups.
8. **A dead official link.** `TOLC_RULES_PAGE` pointed at a 404 (case-sensitive path).

**Checked and sound:** all 73 sitemap URLs answer 200, self-canonical, indexable; reciprocal hreflang; private English routes `noindex` and disallowed; structured data limited to Organization, WebSite, BreadcrumbList, WebPage and Article, all matching visible content. Google retired FAQ rich results (deprecated May 2026) and practice-problem results (deprecated November 2025), and Education Q&A is for flashcards only, so no question markup was added.

**Hypotheses, not proven:** zero clicks at positions 6.2 and 6.5 may reflect snippets that did not match what 2027/28 applicants want (the TIL snippet promised the format of a retiring test), but 37–48 impressions over six days cannot separate that from chance. The preparation plan's position (47.6) is more likely competition and page depth than a technical issue.

**Not examined:** query-level Search Console data (the session's browser is not signed in to Google; nothing was entered), field Core Web Vitals (no CrUX data), Bing.

## 4. Limitations and decisions for the owners

- **Not deployed.** Deploy through the usual preview-first `npm run cf:deploy` from a clean checkout once committed; then submit the three changed URLs for indexing in Search Console.
- **PoliTo facts are provisional by nature.** The page says what is not published. Re-check every two weeks (CONTENT-MAINTENANCE.md); when the calls appear, decide whether TIL practice stays open.
- **The TOLC and PoliTo pages are English.** The demand is largely Italian; translating needs a fluent reviewer (EXAM-INTENT-MAP.md §3, item 2).
- **CEnT-S is not covered.** The pages say so instead of sending CEnT-S candidates to TOLC practice.
- Not fixed, low impact: four localized Bocconi titles over 70 characters, pricing descriptions over 160, the home page's TOLC tab with no sample question (its sample API answers 404 by design).
