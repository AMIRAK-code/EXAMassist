# Relevant independent mentions: outreach plan

Researched 2 November 2026. **Nothing has been sent, posted or submitted**, no account was created, and no link was bought or traded. Each message below goes out only when an owner has read it, approves it, and sends it from their own mailbox. This repository is public, so the plan names organisations and the pages where their contacts are published, not individual teachers' names or addresses; those are in the owners' working notes.

## Principles

- Say in the first lines that the sender works on UniExamer. Never imply affiliation with Bocconi, CISIA or PoliTo, or that anyone has reviewed or endorsed the material.
- Say that the samples are original and AI-assisted and that the site has paid plans. Do not name reviewers or say the material was "reviewed by teachers" unless the reviewers agreed to be named (docs/review/README.md).
- No link requests in exchange for anything, no score predictions, one message per destination, no follow-up chains.
- A correction is sent for the reader's sake; it carries no link to us unless the link is the context.
- Send only after the branch is deployed (the Italian TOLC page and the TOLC-F samples exist only from then).

## How to tell whether it worked (no evidence yet)

Log each message in a private sheet: date, destination, owner who sent it, reply. Mentions are confirmed only by finding the page that links or cites us (check by hand, and Search Console → Links → Top linking sites at the 28-day report). A correction that the page owner made counts as a success even with no link. Do not report outreach as done until messages are actually sent, and do not report a mention until it is visible.
